The problem

Three consultants, three reports. Nobody owns the whole picture.

On most projects the surveyor, the engineer and the project manager work in separate silos. Information gets lost between them and the client pays for the gaps.

French & Co. brings the three disciplines into one consultancy, so decisions are made with the full picture from day one.
