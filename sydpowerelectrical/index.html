Solar and batteries

Make the sun work after dark.

01 · Capture

Your roof makes the power.

Solar panels generate through the day, but without storage most of it is exported for a few cents.

02 · Store

A battery keeps it for later.

A home battery stores the excess so you can run the house at night on your own sunshine instead of the grid.

03 · Expand

Grow the system as you go.

Already have a Powerwall 2 or 3? Expansion units and add-on batteries can increase your storage without starting again.

04 · Protect

Stay on when the grid goes off.

With backup circuits and an automatic transfer switch, the fridge, lights and Wi-Fi keep running through an outage.
