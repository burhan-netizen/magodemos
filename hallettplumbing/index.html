How we find leaks

Non-invasive testing, pinpoint results.

01 · Thermal

Thermal imaging

Temperature differences reveal water travelling behind walls and floors.

02 · Moisture

Moisture readings

Meters confirm where water is sitting and how far it has spread.

03 · Acoustic

Acoustic listening

Sensitive equipment picks up the sound of escaping water in pressurised pipes.

04 · Tracer gas

Tracer gas

For the hardest leaks, a safe gas is introduced and our detector finds exactly where it escapes.
